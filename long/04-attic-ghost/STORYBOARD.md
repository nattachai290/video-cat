# Pumpkin & Pip: The Attic Ghost
## Storyboard v2

**Format:** 1920×1080 · 24fps · 200 seconds  
**Structure:** 3 parts × 60 seconds + 20-second end screen  
**Language:** English dialogue, English karaoke subtitles  
**Style:** Cute cartoon comedy, spooky but family-friendly  
**Pacing:** Visual gag, reaction, or meaningful action every 3–5 seconds. No static shot longer than 3 seconds without action.

---

## Story Premise

On a stormy night, Pumpkin and Pip hear mysterious noises coming from the attic.

Pumpkin is terrified but refuses to admit it. Pip decides to investigate. They encounter several spooky silhouettes and strange clues, eventually discovering that the "ghost" is actually a tiny, cold baby owl trapped in the attic.

They help the owl, repair the broken window, and send it safely home. But the next night, the little owl returns—with its entire family.

---

## Characters

- **Pumpkin:** Orange cat wearing a red vest. Easily frightened but tries to act brave.
- **Pip:** Small gray mouse wearing a green scarf. Curious, confident, and adventurous.
- **Hoot:** Tiny fluffy white baby owl. Innocent, cold, and making mysterious "hoo" sounds.
- **Owl Family:** Two adult owls and several baby owls.

## Audio Direction

- Background music: playful spooky comedy, not horror.
- Storm: thunder and rain during Parts 1–2.
- Attic: creaking wood, dust sneeze, cobweb, footsteps.
- Scares: short comedic stings, not loud horror impacts.
- Reveal: transition to warm, gentle music.
- Ending: cheerful, heartwarming musical finish.
- Dialogue: clear English voices with expressive delivery.
- Subtitles: English karaoke-style subtitles, synchronized to dialogue.
