# Pumpkin & Pip: Beach Day
## Storyboard v2 — The Great Beach Adventure

**Format:** 1920×1080 · 24fps · 200 seconds  
**Structure:** 3 Parts × 60 seconds + 20-second End Screen  
**Language:** English dialogue, English karaoke subtitles  
**Genre:** Beach comedy · Chase · Sandcastle adventure  
**Visual pacing:** A meaningful action, reaction, or visual gag every 3–5 seconds.  
**Production:** Contact sheets must be reviewed before full video rendering.

---

# 1. Story Overview

Pumpkin and Pip spend a day at the beach.

Pip dreams of building the biggest sandcastle ever. Pumpkin just wants to relax under a beach umbrella, far away from the water.

Their peaceful day quickly turns chaotic when Gus, a cheeky seagull, steals Pip's cracker.

While Pip chases Gus, a grumpy little hermit crab named Shelly moves into Pip's sandcastle and refuses to leave.

Pip and Pumpkin build an even bigger castle to outsmart Shelly. But Gus returns and accidentally drops the stolen cracker into the ocean.

Pumpkin must overcome his fear of water to retrieve it. With Pip's help, a ridiculous shark costume, and a pizza-box surfboard, Pumpkin becomes the unlikely hero of the beach.

By sunset, the four unlikely friends share the remaining crackers.

# 2. Character Guide

## Pumpkin
- Orange cat wearing a red vest.
- Beach accessories: oversized sunglasses, wide-brimmed sun hat, yellow duck-shaped swim ring.
- Hates getting wet and is easily frightened by water.
- Dramatic facial expressions and exaggerated reactions.
- Tries to look cool even when terrified.
- Character arc: reluctant beach visitor → frightened hero → proud but exhausted swimmer.

## Pip
- Small gray mouse wearing a green scarf.
- Carries a tiny bucket and shovel.
- Energetic, imaginative, and determined.
- Loves building sandcastles.
- Sometimes gets carried away with his own plans.
- Supports Pumpkin when he needs courage.

## Gus — The Seagull
- Large seagull with white feathers, gray wings, and orange beak.
- Confident, cheeky, and slightly lazy.
- Never speaks.
- Communicates through head tilts, wing gestures, smug expressions, and loud "KAA!" calls.
- Often walks away slowly after stealing something.

## Shelly — The Hermit Crab
- Tiny hermit crab with oversized claws and a colorful shell.
- Grumpy, territorial, and stubborn.
- Never speaks.
- Communicates through claw clicks, sideways walking, and exaggerated defensive poses.
- Treats the sandcastle as a real home.

# 3. Environment & Visual Direction

## Main Beach
- Front-facing composition.
- Golden yellow sand in the foreground.
- Blue ocean in the middle.
- Bright sky in the upper background.
- Red-and-white striped beach umbrella.
- Beach blanket and picnic basket.
- Gentle moving waves throughout the scene.

## Ocean / Surf Zone
- Clear blue water.
- Simple stylized wave shapes.
- One large curling wave with a readable tunnel.
- Avoid overly realistic water simulation.
- Keep characters clearly visible inside the wave.

## Sunset
- Same beach environment.
- Sky transitions from blue to orange and pink.
- Warm reflected light on the characters.
- Gentle waves and long shadows.

**Visual rule:** Keep backgrounds simple enough for the characters and their expressions to remain the main focus.

# PART 1 — BEACH TROUBLE
**Time:** 00:00–01:00

**Goal:** Introduce the beach, establish Pumpkin's fear of sand and water, introduce Gus, and end with Shelly stealing the castle.

| Beat | Time | Visual / Action | Dialogue / Audio |
|---|---|---|---|

| 1-2 | 05–10 | Pumpkin puts down the umbrella, blanket, basket, and duck ring. He shakes sand off his paws, but more sand sticks to his fur. | Pumpkin: "Sand. In my fur. Forever." |

| 1-4 | 15–20 | Pip starts building a sandcastle with intense concentration. Pumpkin relaxes under the umbrella, sunglasses on. | Relaxing beach music |

**Part 1 ending:** Pip stares at his stolen castle. Pumpkin looks at Shelly, then at Pip.

| Beat | Time | Visual / Action | Dialogue / Audio |

| 3-5 | 140–145 | Pip places the pizza-box surfboard in the water. Pumpkin slowly climbs aboard, trembling. | Nervous music |
| 3-6 | 145–150 | A wave lifts the board. Pumpkin's eyes widen as he starts sliding forward. | Rising surf SFX |
| 3-7 | 150–155 | Pumpkin wobbles wildly, arms stretched out. Pip runs along the shallow edge cheering him on. | Pip: "You can do it!" |

# 6. Animation & Continuity Rules

## Character Consistency
- Pumpkin always wears the red vest, including under the shark costume.
- Pip always wears the green scarf.
- Pumpkin's duck ring remains visible during the ocean sequence.
- Gus remains larger than Pip and Shelly.
- Shelly remains small, with oversized claws.

## Story Continuity
- The cracker is introduced in Part 1 as the sandcastle flag.
- Gus steals it in Part 1.
- Gus drops it into the ocean in Part 2.
- The cracker floats visibly away before Pumpkin decides to retrieve it.
- The shark costume is visible in the picnic basket before Part 3.
- The empty pizza box is established as part of the picnic supplies before it becomes a surfboard.
- The cracker remains in Pumpkin's mouth when he reaches the shore.
- The four characters share the recovered cracker at sunset.

## Animation Rules
- Gus must move with confident, lazy body language.
- Shelly's claw gestures must be readable at mobile resolution.
- Pumpkin's fear of water should be expressed through facial expressions and body language.
- Ocean movement should be stylized and simple, not photorealistic.
- The wave tunnel must keep Pumpkin and Pip visible.
- Avoid character clipping, especially with the shark costume, duck ring, and pizza-box surfboard.
