# Snackatoon: channel description

## Description (paste into YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info → Description)

Bite-size cartoons for your snack break! 🍪

Snackatoon is a home for short, silly, original cartoon stories, each one small enough to watch in under a minute and
fun enough to watch again.

Meet the first residents:
🐱 Pumpkin: a round orange cat in a vest that's one size too small. Big appetite, bigger ideas, terrible plans.
🐭 Pip: a tiny grey mouse with a green scarf and a cracker. Always calm, always right, always snacking.

More characters and more kinds of stories are on the way: comedy, fables, little adventures and cozy moments.

Every frame, voice, sound effect and note of music is made from scratch, with code and AI tools.

Subscribe and grab a snack. 🍩
